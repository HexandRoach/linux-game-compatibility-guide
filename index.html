Compatibility status guide

Every game report uses one clear status based on our latest community testing. Game patches, Proton versions, GPU drivers, launchers, and anti-cheat updates can change a game’s status, so reports should be retested.

Excellent

Works out of the box

Installs, launches, and plays reliably with little or no additional setup.

Playable

Minor setup needed

Works after a small change such as selecting Proton or adding launch options.

Limited

Major caveats

Runs, but important features like multiplayer, performance, or stability may suffer.

Unsupported

Not reliably playable

Fails to launch, crashes, or has a blocker such as unsupported anti-cheat.

Untested

Needs a report

No current verified Linux or Steam Deck test is available yet.
